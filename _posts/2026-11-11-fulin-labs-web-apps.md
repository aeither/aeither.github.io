---
title: "Fulin Labs: four small web apps"
---

Fulin Labs is an indie studio building small, focused web apps.

- [HSK Studio](https://hsk.fulinlabs.com/): HSK vocabulary lists, flashcards and a Chinese text analyzer.
- [Fulin Flashcard](https://flashcard.fulinlabs.com/): fast spaced-repetition flashcards for any subject.
- [ShortLink](https://s.fulinlabs.com/): short links with a UTM builder and click analytics.
- [SchedulePost](https://schedulepost.fulinlabs.com/): schedule Reels, TikTok and other short videos from one queue.

Guides and free tools live at [fulinlabs.com](https://fulinlabs.com/).
